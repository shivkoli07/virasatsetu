Ganesh Chaturthi

Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturthi • Maharashtra & Pan-India
Panchang Tithi: Shukla Paksha, Swati Nakshatra. Auspicious advent of Vighnaharta Ganesha.

Ritual & Cultural Practices

Installation of handcrafted Shadu Mati clay idols, daily aarti with Dhol-Tasha folk drums, and Vedic chant recitations.

Seasonal Cuisines & Sacred Attire

Cuisine: Steamed Ukadiche Modak with jaggery, fresh coconut, and pure ghee.
Attire: Traditional pure silk Dhoti-Kurta with Angavastram and Nauvari Paithani sarees.
