Kailasa Rock-Cut Temple

Ellora, Maharashtra Rashtrakuta Dynasty • 8th C. Monolithic Basalt

Carved top-down from a single volcanic basalt cliff, removing over 200,000 tonnes of rock without scaffolding or mortar seams. Commissioned under Rashtrakuta King Krishna I, the monument features a multi-tiered Dravidian vimana spire and monolithic life-sized elephants.

Architectural Feat: Sculptors worked simultaneously from the summit downwards, following strict proportions recorded in ancient Vastu Shastra treaties.
