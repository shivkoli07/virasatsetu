Kailasa Rock-Cut Temple

Maharashtra Rashtrakuta Dynasty • 8th C. Monolith Architecture

Cave 16 at Ellora represents the undisputed zenith of rock-cut monolithic craftsmanship. Commissioned under Rashtrakuta King Krishna I in the 8th century, master masons removed over 200,000 tonnes of volcanic basalt rock top-down from a single escarpment without a single joint seam or structural mortar.

GPS: 20.0238° N, 75.1791° E UNESCO World Heritage
